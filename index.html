Help & Support

Knowledge Base & Assistance
💡

On the registration screen, enter your chosen username, email, and password. PingX automatically generates a unique, pseudonymous 8-digit ID and an instant activation OTP code. Once verified, you sign in with your 8-digit ID anytime.

Tap the purple floating action button (+) on the main Chats screen. Enter the recipient's 8-digit PingX ID and a brief purpose or reason (e.g. "Project collaboration"). Senders cannot spam messages until the recipient reviews and accepts the connection request.

PingX uses randomized 8-digit IDs instead of requiring your phone number. We do not harvest contacts, collect GPS coordinates, or embed ad trackers. All network transport is secured by TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.

Inside any open chat, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top header and select "Clear Messages". You can also clear all stored local chat messages across all conversations from Privacy Center > User Data Sovereignty > Clear Chat History.

Open the chat with the user, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top header, and choose "Block User". You will be asked to confirm. Blocked users cannot send you messages or connection requests. You can view and unblock contacts anytime in Settings > Blocked Users.

Tap the three dots (⋮) in the chat header and choose "Report User". Select the category (Spam, Harassment, Inappropriate Content, Impersonation, or Other), provide any optional details, and submit. You can also block the user simultaneously.

Your 8-digit ID is permanent for your account. If you lose your credentials, contact our support team below using your registered email address to verify account recovery.

Go to the Privacy Center. Tap "Export Personal Data" to immediately download a full JSON archive of your profile, messages, active sessions, and requests. To permanently delete your account, choose "Delete PingX Account" and complete the verification prompt.
